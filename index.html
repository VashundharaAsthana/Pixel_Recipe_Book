Pancakes

Steps

  1. Mix ingredients into batter.
  2. Heat pan.
  3. Pour batter.
  4. Flip when bubbles appear

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Milk
  • Egg
  • Sugar
  • Butter

Instant Ramen

Steps

  1. Boil water.
  2. Add noodles.
  3. Add seasoning
  4. Add egg and cook

Ingredients

  • Instant noodles.
  • Egg
  • Spring onion.
  • Seasoning Packet

Sandwich

Steps

  1. Butter the bread
  2. Add cheese slice
  3. Toast on pan

Ingredients

  • Bread
  • Cheese
  • Butter

Tomato Soup

Steps

  1. Boil tomatoes(5-7min).
  2. Blend into pureee.
  3. Heat pan with butter.
  4. Add seasoning.

Ingredients

  • Tomates
  • Garlic
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Butter or oil

Sunny Side Up

Steps

  1. Heat pan and add butter.
  2. Crack the egg in pan
  3. Cook the egg without flipping.
  4. Wait until egg white sets.
  5. Add salt and pepper

Ingredients

  • 1 egg
  • Butter
  • Salt
  • Pepper

Avocado Toast

Steps

  1. Toast bread.
  2. Mash avocado
  3. Spread and add lemon.
  4. Season with salt and pepper.

Ingredients

  • Bread
  • Avocado
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Lemon

Scrambled Eggs

Steps

  1. Beat eggs.
  2. Heat butter in pan
  3. Add egg and salt.
  4. Cook slowly.
  5. Stir continuously.

Ingredients

  • 1 egg
  • Butter
  • Salt

Fried Rice

Steps

  1. Heat pan and add butter.
  2. Scramble the egg.
  3. Add rice.
  4. Add soy sauce salt pepper.
  5. Stir fry.

Ingredients

  • 1 egg
  • Butter
  • Salt
  • Soy Sauce

Pudding

Steps

  1. Heat mil.
  2. Mix sugar+cornstarch.
  3. Add milk+vanilla extract,stir.
  4. Cook until thick.
  5. Cool and serve.

Ingredients

  • Milk
  • Sugar
  • Cornstarch
  • Vanilla extract

Salad

Steps

  1. Chop veggies.
  2. Mix in a bowl.
  3. Add dressing.
  4. Toss.

Ingredients

  • Lettuce
  • Tomato
  • Cucumber
  • Dressing
